## Electronics Recycling at OWWL HQ Through November 24

There are only three more chances to drop off e-waste for recycling in 2026. Drop-off days are Tuesdays and Thursdays from 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM, through November 24, which leaves today, Thursday, and next Tuesday.

OWWL accepts old library computers, monitors, printers, network equipment (routers, firewalls, and modems), and accessories such as mice, keyboards, and cables. Please do not send electronics for recycling through delivery.

When you arrive, park behind the building next to the garage door and notify the CANS staff that you are here. A sign on the garage door lists a phone number to call.

Some of the computers and monitors brought in for recycling may be kept and used for System-wide programs. If you prefer that your equipment go directly to e-waste recycling, please tell us by message or at the time of drop-off.

If you need help transporting equipment to the office, or if the drop-off days and times do not work for you, send a message to `support`.

## Multifactor Authentication for Member Libraries

Multifactor authentication (MFA, sometimes called two-factor authentication or 2FA) is a way to secure online accounts. In addition to a username and password, the account requires a second method of verification, such as a code from an authenticator app. Even if someone learns a password, they cannot get into the account without that second method, so people who use MFA are much less likely to be hacked.

OWWL Docs has a [Multifactor Authentication page](https://docs.owwl.org/Members/MultifactorAuthentication) for member libraries. It explains the available methods for Zimbra and Evergreen, links to setup instructions for each, and covers troubleshooting. OWWL does not allow text messages (SMS) as an MFA method.

We talked about this at OWWLDAC, and the page above is the place to start for questions.

## RRLC Grant Opportunities

The Rochester Regional Library Council's grant season is here. Each grant has its own page with the requirements and deadlines, and the full list is on the [RRLC funding opportunities page](https://rrlc.org/services/grant-opportunities/).

- [Action & Innovation Grant](https://rrlc.org/services/grant-opportunities/action-innovation-grant/) supports projects that take action on issues affecting the community your organization serves.
- [Collections & Access Grant](https://rrlc.org/services/grant-opportunities/collections-access-grant/) supports member projects that improve access to collections.
- [Capacity Building Grant](https://rrlc.org/services/grant-opportunities/capacity-building-grant/) supports projects that strengthen internal operations, sustainability, and effectiveness.
- [Internship Grant](https://rrlc.org/services/grant-opportunities/internship-grants/) helps introduce students to careers in libraries, archives, museums, and cultural heritage organizations by funding meaningful work experience.
- [Special Project Grant](https://rrlc.org/services/grant-opportunities/special-project-grant/) supports member projects that help an organization fulfill its mission, with first priority for projects that could affect the larger RRLC community.

## NYLA 2027 Developing Leaders Program

The New York Library Association (NYLA) is accepting applications for the 2027 Developing Leaders Program. The program aims to connect, educate, and empower rising leaders in the library profession. It runs for six months, from January to June, and is designed for library professionals with at least three years of experience who want to explore and strengthen their leadership skills.

Topics include advocacy, communications, crisis management and preparedness, equity, diversity, and inclusion, human resources, marketing and personal brand, and project and time management, among others.

[Apply through NYLA](https://www.nyla.org/developing-leaders-program). The application deadline is January 8, 2027.
