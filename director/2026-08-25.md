## George Eastman Museum "Check Us Out" Passes

OWWL has partnered with the George Eastman Museum (GEM) on a "Check Us Out" museum pass program. GEM is providing 80 passes for member libraries to check out to patrons, each good for buy-one-get-one admission, valid through June 2027.

If your library wants to participate, respond to the opt-in form below to request your passes. Most libraries will receive 2; smaller libraries will receive 1. If you'd like more than 2, let us know and we'll see what's available once distribution is finalized.

[Opt in for GEM "Check Us Out" Passes](https://forms.gle/4dNmcrucycSL3dw96)

Checkout tracking details will follow once the distribution plan is finalized.

Questions? Reach out to me.

## What's New in Libby

Libby adds new features on an ongoing basis. Here's what's new recently.

- Four County Library System is now a partner library through our Reciprocal Lending Agreement (joining Finger Lakes Library System and Southern Tier Library System).
- Libby automatically checks title availability at every partner library when you search, rather than requiring a separate search at each one.
- A restructured menu screen makes room for the Partner Libraries section.
- Kindle users who return a loan are now taken to their Kindle shelf to finish the return.

Partner library updates like this one show up automatically in the Libby app. There's nothing your library or your patrons need to do to see them.

## Supplemental NYLA Conference Scholarship: Applications Open

OWWL Library System is offering supplemental scholarships of up to $500 to help cover the cost of attending the [2026 NYLA Annual Conference](https://www.nyla.org/registration-information), November 4-7 in Saratoga Springs.

This scholarship is supplemental. It can help offset costs such as

- Conference registration fees
- Up to three nights' lodging at the conference hotel rate, excluding sales tax
- Round-trip mileage between your library and the conference hotel

To apply, you need to be an employee of an OWWL Library System member library in good standing. Awardees arrange and pay for their own registration, hotel, and transportation, then submit documentation for reimbursement after the event, following the guidelines in the Financial Support Policy.

[Apply for the NYLA Conference Scholarship](https://forms.gle/oom193h3jERxj1576)

The application deadline is September 4, 2026.

Please pass this along to your staff as well, since any employee of a member library in good standing is eligible to apply.

Questions? Contact Ron.

### Consumer Reports and Kanopy Renewals

On August 17, I emailed all directors proposing a change to how Kanopy and Consumer Reports are funded starting in 2027. The System can no longer fully cover both out of its operating budget, so I'm bringing the question to a vote at this meeting: whether libraries take on the cost going forward. This follows the bylaw amendment OWWLDAC approved earlier this year, which requires a two-thirds vote (28 directors) before a cost like this gets billed out to all 42 libraries.

If the vote passes, both databases are renewed in October 2026, and libraries are billed in 2027 for the 2028 cost. If it doesn't pass, the affected service ends when its current contract runs out in October 2026.

Consumer Reports and Kanopy are not packaged together. Each is its own decision, so your library can support continuing one without the other.

My August 17 email included your library's estimated cost for each database, based on population served. Take another look at those figures before the meeting, and let me know if you'd like them resent.

## Little Pim Renewal for 2026-2027

Mango Languages is on a three-year contract that the System pays for directly. Little Pim, the ages 0-5 add-on several member libraries asked us to add, runs on its own one-year term and is up for renewal.

OWWLDAC approved adding Little Pim to the Mango contract at its March 6, 2026 meeting.

> Shamika Fusco moves to approve the addition of Little Pim to our current Mango contract. Tanya Taylor seconds, and the motion passed unanimously.

Mango has quoted the 2026-2027 renewal at $2,025, due around November 1, 2026, shared among all 42 member libraries.

Since Little Pim launched in March, system-wide usage has been modest: 78 total uses and just over 6 hours of combined learning time across March through July 2026 (usage isn't broken out by library, the same as Kanopy). Monthly totals: 3 uses in March, 26 in April, 35 in May, 8 in June, and 6 in July. 

I expect this to renew as planned, since it's already an active subscription libraries agreed to at the time it was added. I'll add it to the OWWLDAC agenda in case it needs any additional discussion or if OWWLDAC has changed its mind on the subscription. If your library has concerns before then, let me know.
